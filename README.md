## English

**Last updated: October 18, 2026**

Choicewise ("the app") is a decision-reflection tool published by Vuqubey. The app is free and is supported by advertising.

**What stays on your device.** The text you enter (decision descriptions, options, ratings), your recent decisions and your language choice are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, so you can come back to them. We do not receive this data, it is never uploaded, and it is excluded from Android cloud backups. You can delete a saved decision inside the app; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

**We do not collect personal data ourselves.** There are no accounts and we run no servers that receive your data.

**Advertising (Google AdMob).** The app shows banner and full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and provide analytics, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and use: your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches and taps), diagnostic information about app and SDK performance, and device and account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. This is the only data that leaves your device. It is sent to Google and handled under Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Your decisions, ratings and the text you type are never shared with advertisers.

**Your ad choices.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice using Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can review or change it at any time under "Ad privacy settings" on the home screen. Ads may be personalized where you consent or where the law allows. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Without consent, where it is required, no ads are shown.

**Internet access and permissions.** The app connects to the internet only to load ads. It uses the permissions needed for this (internet and network state, and the Google advertising ID permission) and the vibration permission for haptic feedback. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. Ad content is limited to a general audience (PG) rating.

**Not professional advice.** Choicewise is a reflection tool. Its results are not legal, medical, financial or other professional advice.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

**Contact.** vuqubey@gmail.com
